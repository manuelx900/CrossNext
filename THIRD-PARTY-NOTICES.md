# Third-party notices

CrossNext derives from CrossPlay and is distributed under MIT (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). It also ships other people's work,
some of it compiled into the firmware where a licence file cannot travel beside
it. This is that list.

Where a licence is reproduced in full elsewhere in the tree, this file points at
it rather than copying it twice.

## Content on the SD card and in the browser build

**xkcd**, by Randall Munroe, [CC BY-NC 2.5](https://xkcd.com/license.html). The
device fetches comics from xkcd.com, and the browser demo at
crossplay.ma-r-s.com carries a forty-comic slice packed from there. The licence
permits non-commercial redistribution with attribution; nothing here is sold.

**Connections** puzzles are the New York Times'. CrossPlay ships none of them.
The app fetches a board at runtime, at the user's request, from a third-party
mirror.

**Hacker News** content belongs to its posters. The browser demo freezes a
snapshot of the front page, one thread's comments and two articles so the app
can be tried offline; the device itself fetches live from the public
[Algolia API](https://hn.algolia.com/api).

**Trivia** questions are built from the
[Jeopardy! clue dataset](https://github.com/jwolle1/jeopardy_clue_dataset), a
community transcription of aired clues. JEOPARDY! is a trademark of Jeopardy
Productions, Inc.; CrossPlay is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by
them, and the app is not the show. How the pack is cut and ranked is
[docs/trivia-curation.md](docs/trivia-curation.md).

**Alice's Adventures in Wonderland** by Lewis Carroll, on the browser demo's SD
card, from [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11). Public
domain.

The retired Mandarin simulator deck and its KaiTi fonts are excluded from the
CrossNext source export and browser card because their redistribution grant was
not established. Local development copies are not part of the release.

## Games

Where a game on the shelf carries the name of a published game, that name is
its owner's trademark. CrossPlay implements the games; it is not affiliated
with, endorsed by or sponsored by any of their owners. Game mechanics are not
copyrightable; the names are used to say what the thing is.

Named rather than enumerated on purpose. The list here read "Jaipur, Insider,
Murdle, Battleship and Connections" and had not grown with the shelf: it was
missing Knucklebones, Sea Salt & Paper, Toy Battle, Yahtzee and Wavelength, all
of which ship. A list of trademarks is exactly the kind that goes stale
silently, and being incomplete is the one way this notice can do harm.

## CrossNext references and branding

The integration base is CrossPlay 1.14.2 / CrossPoint 1.6.5; original copyright
and permission notices remain in LICENSE. CrossInk (uxjulia/CrossInk, MIT) was
used as an interface reference and as the source of the unmodified optional
Bitter/Lexend Deca font files, whose OFL notices remain with them. Its MIT notice
is retained in licenses/CrossInk-MIT.txt. VCodex (franssjz/cpr-vcodex) was inspected
as a reading-statistics and vocabulary workflow reference; this does not imply
that its engine or cache was imported. See docs/apps/words.md for that distinction.

The CrossNext logo geometry was supplied by this fork's maintainer. The SVG and
bitmap are generated from the same monochrome pixel grid. Additional light,
warmth, rotation and wallpaper-selection symbols are local MIT interface art;
see assets_local/extra-icons/NOTICE. README presentation images are supplied by
the maintainer and include earlier screenshots, as documented beside the images.

Binary packages retain full available dependency/font/icon license files under
licenses/ with their source-tree paths. Resource packages retain their own
font, dictionary and illustration notices. Keep those notices with redistribution.
