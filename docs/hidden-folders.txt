Hidden folders / Carpetas ocultas

EN: In Files, hold a custom folder and choose Hide. The folder keeps its name,
path and contents; apps can still access it directly. Settings > Library > Show
hidden files reveals hidden folders. Hold one and choose Show to remove its
custom hidden state. Resource folders (sleep, fonts, languages, dictionaries,
notes and other app resources) and dot folders are hidden by default; use the
settings toggle to browse them.

ES: En Archivos, mantén pulsada una carpeta y elige Ocultar. Conserva su nombre,
ruta y contenido; las apps siguen accediendo directamente. Ajustes > Biblioteca >
Mostrar archivos ocultos permite verla. Mantén pulsada una carpeta oculta y elige
Mostrar para quitar su marca. Las carpetas de recursos y las que empiezan con
punto se muestran mediante el ajuste general.

A custom hidden folder contains a one-byte .crossnext-hidden marker. Hiding
never moves, renames or deletes books. The explorer checks this marker only for
folders while hidden items are disabled; the library inventory is unchanged.
