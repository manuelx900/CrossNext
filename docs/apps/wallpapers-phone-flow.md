# Wallpaper sources

CrossNext Wallpapers is a local microSD gallery. CrossPlay's wallpaper pack
download, QR upload, Your phone and Live screens have been removed, together
with their Activity routes and Live sleep/wake checks. No pairing or Live radio
job runs from Wallpapers, suspension or selecting a note as the sleep screen.

Copy supported BMP, PNG or JPEG images into `/sleep`, `/WALLPAPER` or `/wallpapers`
on the card, then open Wallpapers. Preparation reports progress and the gallery
reuses valid thumbnails. Unreadable files cannot be selected manually or with All.

The header selection icon opens multi-selection. All toggles the usable set;
Done asks before changing suspension backgrounds. A tap previews, and holding
an image opens its local actions. The gallery and its messages use Jersey.
