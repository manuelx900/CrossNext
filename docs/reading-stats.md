# Reading statistics and Home menu

The current interface has four tabs: Today, Stats, Calendar and Books, with day browsing, hours/minutes cards, reference illustrations, icon metrics, consistency, charts and completed-book history. See [the current refinement profile](study-suite.md#ui-and-game-refinement-profile-october-2026). Older three-tab descriptions below refer to the earlier arrangement.

Home starts with Browse Files, Recent Books, Reading Stats, Games, Apps and Settings, in that order. Open Settings > System > Home Menu to rename a row or show/hide rows. At most six may be enabled. Settings is always enabled and may be renamed. Hide an enabled row before adding File Transfer or the optional Plugins/OPDS row. That services row is available when plugins are installed or OPDS servers are configured. Clearing a custom name restores its translated default; names accept up to 48 UTF-8 bytes. Reorder Menu Items lets you select a visible entry and move it up or down. Settings can move but stays visible. Moves save immediately and apply to every Home layout. Hidden entries retain their relative slots until enabled.

Menu preferences live in `/.crosspoint/home-menu-a.bin` and `home-menu-b.bin`. Writes alternate between checksummed slots and are read back before the active settings change. A failed save leaves the previous configuration selected. Unsupported future formats are preserved and treated as read only.

Reading Stats has four tabs, in order:

- Today: browse individual recorded days and continue reading.
- Stats: lifetime reading time, sessions, average session, qualified forward pages, pages/minute, finished books and consistency.
- Calendar: the selected month, seven daily reading-time bars for the current week and six monthly reading-day bars. Tap a date for day details, a daily bar for day details, or the month heading/monthly bar for month details. Details keep all four tabs, previous/next period controls and Back to Calendar. Daily details include six four-hour time bins; weeks include daily minutes; months include reading days and consistency through the selected period's last date (or today for the current month). Future dates are unavailable. Week boundaries follow the Calendar app's week-start preference; week numbers use ISO 8601's Monday-based numbering, including for a Sunday-start display. The calendar browses the latest 730 days; detail totals are streamed from the full journal, including an entire boundary week/month. Filled days represent 30+ minutes, dithered days with positive reading under 30 minutes (including subminute sessions), and unfilled days no reading; a double border marks today.
- Books: Finished and Recent sections. Finished contains every first completion, newest completion first. Recent keeps a bounded window of up to ten unfinished documents, ordered by journal append order. Completing a document removes it from that window. Re-reading moves a document to the top and replaces its cumulative snapshot without adding a duplicate. Both categories show five books per page in portrait and three books per page in landscape, each in a single column. Compact total minutes appear above the min label; additional digits shrink to fit. Select a card for title/status, progress, total reading time, sessions, average session, pages/minute, estimated time left, first reading date and finish date (or last-read date for an unfinished book). The detail is refreshed from the latest valid cumulative snapshot. No reading records means no fabricated book statistics.

Left/Right switches tabs on button controls. Up/Down changes the calendar month or selected period, or selects a book; Confirm opens a selected book (or the displayed month's details). Back returns from a period to Calendar or a book detail to its same list/category/page, then to Home. Touch uses visible controls and the shared header Back target. Landscape arranges details in two columns and uses one upper Back control to retain readable metrics. Long book titles may be truncated to the available title area; control labels and metric names use the shared compact type scale.

## What is recorded

The reader's existing `ReaderSession` protocol qualifies a forward page after it has been displayed for at least two seconds. Each page contributes at most 30 minutes. Backward turns, failed turns, refreshes and menu time add no reading pages. A session must accumulate ten seconds and have a trusted device timestamp. Time is active page dwell, rather than the entire period the book remained open. The final page without a subsequent forward turn does not add dwell time. Sync the device clock in Settings before reading.

A session is flushed on leaving the reader or preparing for sleep. The journal records its start timestamp, active duration, pages and final progress. Calendar/time-of-day distribution uses that start plus the active duration, splitting at midnight and four-hour boundaries. A completion belongs to its finish timestamp, including a completion exactly at midnight. Period sessions count sessions with positive reading time overlapping that period; a midnight completion can therefore appear on a day with zero sessions. Pages are apportioned by cumulative integer rounding, preserving their sum across adjacent periods. Pauses inside a session are compressed: the displayed day/time distribution is an estimate when a session spans long inactive gaps. Pages/minute counts rendered forward pages and changes with font/layout; it is not words/minute. A book is completed once recorded reader progress reaches 100%. Re-reading the same document updates its statistics without adding a second finished-book entry. Existing reading before this feature was installed is not fabricated or imported.

## Persistence and resources

The authoritative append-only journal is `/.crosspoint/reading-history/sessions.bin`, with a 16-byte versioned header and 384-byte checksummed records. Every record also holds a cumulative book snapshot, so disposable per-document `.idx` files can be rebuilt. Missing/stale indexes fall back to a sequential scan. A partial final record is discarded on the next append; invalid checksummed records are skipped without a persistent list banner, while I/O errors and unsupported headers stop the scan. A power cut can lose the unflushed session. Previous valid records remain available.

The activity owns one fixed 730-day summary, two bounded period accumulators, ten reusable book snapshots plus one detail snapshot and reusable UI descriptors. These replace the previous second 730-day summary and six-entry page buffer. No journal format change or migration is needed. Month consistency joins the full journal's active-date intervals backward from the selected cutoff; an extra pass joins intervals that were appended out of date order after a clock change. These extra passes do not count aggregates again. It scans at most 32 records per loop so Back remains available while a large journal loads. Reader flush allocates one bounded repository workspace with `makeUniqueNoThrow`, checks allocation failure and releases it on return. No per-book array grows with the library. The journal grows by 384 bytes per session; it currently has no compaction. For ten sessions/day that is about 1.4 MB/year on SD. Clearing EPUB layout/font cache does not remove this folder; deleting the whole `/.crosspoint/` directory does.

The read-only reference firmware in `REFERENCIA FIRMWARE/XPoint-develop` inspired the statistics sections and presentation. The implementation is fork-owned under `src/apps_local/reading`; no build or runtime path requires the reference folder.

## Verification on hardware

Enable File Transfer after hiding a row, rename Settings, reboot and verify the same six rows and dispatch targets. Attempt to enable a seventh or hide Settings. Restore the defaults afterward.

With a synced clock, read several forward pages slowly enough to qualify, leave the reader and check that totals increase. Reopen and sleep to verify persistence without duplicate sessions. Finish a short book, open its entry in Books and inspect its individual statistics; revisit the end and verify the completed count stays unchanged. Compare reading around midnight with the session distribution definition above. Check calendar month boundaries/leap February, empty history, damaged SD handling and a missing index. Preserve a backup before intentionally damaging test data.

Exercise portrait, inverted portrait and both landscape orientations, long/accented titles, selectable dates/weekly/monthly bars, period Back/previous/next controls, first/last history month, both Books categories and their pagination and Back while the loading screen is visible. Repeat reader/statistics entry and exit while monitoring free heap, largest block and task stack high-water marks through debug logging. Browser/host tests do not establish physical e-ink timing or real SD power-loss behavior.

Home shortcuts use the same six-item limit, including the required Settings row. Hide an existing row before adding one. In Show / Hide Menu Items, + Add Game and + Add App open paged selectors of the installed registry. The main list shows only chosen shortcuts. Tap a shortcut there to remove it; Rename Menu Items also renames shortcuts. The shortcut stores folder plus stable item title, so adding/reordering games does not change its target. Opening a shortcut uses the shelf launcher; Back returns directly Home, including after sleep/reboot; launches from Games or Apps still return to their folder. Folder visibility and Home shortcuts are independent.

Menu preferences upgrade HMC1/HMC2 to HMC3 on the next change, preserving old names and visibility. Two 992-byte verified SD slots store at most five shortcut identities, with no per-frame allocation. Thirteen ordered item IDs fit the reserved bytes, so the slots remain 992 bytes. An older firmware preserves HMC3 as an unsupported format instead of overwriting it. Settings remains reachable through the default fallback.


In Stats/Data, tap Total reading time to choose a month or a whole year from 2020 through the current date. The upper Back to Stats/Data block returns to that menu. Previous/next year controls select the picker year; future months are disabled. Details keep Stats/Data selected and retain the existing metrics and period navigation; Back returns to the picker, then Stats/Data. Annual detail charts show books finished per month with integer book counts, attributed to the local completion month. The annual accumulator retains twelve 64-bit reading-time totals and a 366-bit active-date map, and adds twelve 32-bit finished-book counts. The two activity projections add 96 fixed bytes without allocation. The journal and its formats remain unchanged.

The selectable total-reading-time card shows an outlined calendar button in its upper-right corner. Tap the button or anywhere on the card to open the period selector. The button appears only when period selection is available.

The chapter sheet uses four rows per page in portrait (two in landscape), regular document-selector type for chapter names and the existing Roman-numeral type. Long names can wrap to two lines.

The Home-order buttons reuse an activity-owned SDK descriptor while the settings screen exists; it is released with that activity and adds no per-render allocation.
