# Home appearance

The selectable Home appearances are Framed, Carousel, Carousel 2 and Bookshelf.
Carousel 2 emphasizes one cover and six shortcuts; Bookshelf presents a paginated
bookshelf and a larger current-book strip. Their headers are white throughout
the interface. See [Cover themes and Library](cover-themes-library.md) for
filtering, sorting, metadata and orientation behavior.
The indexed book Library, Recent books and MicroSD Files browser remain available.
See [Interface layouts](interface-layouts.md) for current behavior and device checks.

Recent books retains up to 30 opened books. This capacity is independent of the
Home recent-cover count and the Library page size (nine covers in portrait, four
in landscape). Books beyond the capacity leave Recents without being removed
from the microSD or the indexed Library.
