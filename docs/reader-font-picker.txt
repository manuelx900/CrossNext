READER FONT PICKER

In the bottom reading panel, open Font > Font family.
Swipe left for the next page and right for the previous page, or use the pager arrows.
Carousel 2 and Bookshelf show six families per page at X4 Pro screen sizes:
six rows in portrait, or three rows in two columns in landscape.
Framed and Carousel keep their existing density. The UI font and reading font remain independent.
Swiping beyond the first or last page leaves the picker unchanged without starting a display refresh.

DEVICE CHECK
Install at least seven reading font families on the microSD. Open an EPUB and the picker.
Check six choices, page once left, return right, and select a font on the second page.
Repeat in Spanish and English, portrait and landscape, with Carousel 2 and Bookshelf.
The book must not turn a page when swiping inside this picker. Downward dismissal remains available.
