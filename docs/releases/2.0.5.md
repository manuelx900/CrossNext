# CrossNext 2.0.5 — Xteink X4 Pro

## Firmware

- Home Button Gestures now offers None, 100 ms and 200 ms alongside the existing intervals. None sends a single tap straight to Home and remembers your double-tap shortcut for when you re-enable it.
- A single press of the Home button always returns to Home. Double-tap and long-press shortcuts remain customizable.
- Customize the black or white header independently of the theme. PET and Reading Adventure follow your choice, and Framed and Carousel show an outlined Continue Reading button with the white header.
- Carousel 2 now respects the visibility and order of your Home menu items, centers shorter rows, and supports direct shortcuts to installed plugins.
- Home and the Apps and Games lists draw the selected header color directly. The EPUB bottom sheet does less SD work to show the chapter list and reuses the page snapshot when switching overlays or dismissing a picker.
- You can reset the local reading statistics of one book from its statistics screen. Other books and the reading position are preserved.

## Book Preparer for Windows and macOS

- See how many books are already prepared for the selected settings, and continue from that count. Compatible and complete caches are reused, so only remaining work is prepared.
- Prepare EPUB layouts for portrait, landscape or both. Select the same EPUB engine and reading settings on the reader to use those layouts.
- The preparation screen gives more space to the current book and chapter, with a compact progress area and diagnostics that identify books needing attention.
- Interrupted cache updates can be recovered on the next run. An older preparation certificate may need a one-time update before its cache can be reused.

## Updating

- Install the matching CrossNext-Resources.zip for the new language strings and current app resources. The ZIP includes all bundled languages and contains no root images folder.
- The Windows executable is portable. Use the arm64 DMG for Apple Silicon or the x64 DMG for Intel Macs.
- See INSTALL.txt for the correct X4 Pro firmware image and microSD update instructions.
