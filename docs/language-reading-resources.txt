LANGUAGE AND READING RESOURCES

Install the matching firmware and copy languages/ from its resource ZIP to the
microSD root. English and Spanish remain built in. Other interface languages
need their .cplang and the interface fonts in languages/fonts/. Reading fonts
and reading dictionaries are separate resources.

Wordle
------
The optional CPWB v2 banks provide 38 natural languages, including English and
Spanish, with at least 2000 answers per language across the supported word
lengths and larger allowed-guess lists. Answers are selected by corpus frequency;
they are not a guarantee of native editorial quality or universally simple words.
The Ook novelty pack uses its existing English fallback. Missing English/Spanish
banks use their built-in fallback vocabulary. Install these banks with the
firmware supplied alongside them; older firmware cannot read CPWB v2.
The banks are read on demand; opening v2 validates a small header rather than
scanning the entire file. Accessed records have their own checksums.
See languages/wordle/SOURCE.txt and MANIFEST.json for source attribution,
licenses, counts and rebuilding information. These banks replace the older
small starter banks described in the original Wordle documentation.

Book dictionaries
-----------------
In an EPUB, open the bottom reading panel, select More, then Dictionary and
translator. Choose the definition dictionary or translation dictionary separately.
Use system setting follows the global choice. None explicitly disables that
type of lookup for this book. A named choice applies only to this book.
The choices remain after reopening, clearing regenerable book caches, renaming
through Files or automatically moving a finished EPUB into Read.
Preferences live under /.crosspoint/book-dictionaries/ in two small alternating
records. An interrupted write leaves the previous validated record available.
The selector lists up to 128 dictionary folders, each with a name of at most
31 UTF-8 bytes. Removing a selected dictionary does not silently change the
selection; reinstall it or choose another dictionary.

Preparation and performance
---------------------------
Settings > Reading > Prepare full book on opening is optional and off by default.
When enabled, opening a book shows a compact Indexing popup with percent progress
over the previous screen. Framed/Carousel use a black square popup; Carousel 2/
Bookshelf use a white rounded popup. Cancel preserves completed preparation work.
The separate Prepare books action still prepares the whole card. Font selection
pages also accept horizontal swipes. Selecting exactly one font changes the
default reading font when preparation starts; the screen explains this behavior.
More fonts consume more time and microSD space. Cached layouts can be reused
until reading layout, source, fonts or cache formats change, or caches are removed.
Percent represents preparation units, not an estimated remaining time.

Technical checks validate translation coverage, formatting arguments, fonts,
keyboards and game-bank access. Assisted translations and frequency-ranked words
still need native-speaker review. Simulator timings do not establish physical
X4 Pro speed; test actual book opening, page changes and menus on the device.
